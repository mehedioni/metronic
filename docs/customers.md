# Customers

## Why customers are their own table

Orders keep `customer_name`, `customer_email` and `customer_phone` of their
own, and also point at a customer through a nullable `orders.customer_id`.
Both exist on purpose:

- the **customer record** is the current contact detail;
- the **order columns** are what those details were when the order was placed.

Editing a customer's phone number must not rewrite what a year-old invoice
says. `Customer::orderSnapshot()` is what `OrderService` copies onto a new
order, and anything the caller sends explicitly wins over the snapshot.

`customer_id` is nullable because a store takes walk-in sales. An order with a
name and no customer record is valid; `customer_name` is only required when no
`customer_id` is given (`required_without:customer_id`).

The FK is `nullOnDelete`: deleting a customer must not take their orders with
it, and the order still reads correctly from its own snapshot.

## Spend figures are never stored

`orders`, `total`, `last_order_at` and the average order value are summed from
the orders table on read — in `CustomerService::paginate()` as query
aggregates, and in `history()` for the detail screen. Nothing is cached on the
customer row, so a figure cannot drift from the orders behind it.

Cancelled orders never count towards spend. `Customer::billableOrders()` is the
relation that enforces it, and both the list and the detail screen go through
it. The cancelled count is reported separately.

Because the aggregates are selected as columns, they are also sortable:
`orders_count`, `total_spent` and `last_order_at` are on
`CustomerService::SORTABLE`.

## Deletion

A customer with any order is never deleted — `CustomerService::delete()`
raises `RestrictedDeletionException`, which reaches the UI as an `inventory`
field error. Deactivate them instead (`PATCH inventory.customers.status`),
which leaves every order resolving to a customer.

A customer with no orders is soft-deleted, and the unique indexes on `code`
and `email` ignore soft-deleted rows so the values can be reused.

## Codes

`code` is generated by `DocumentNumberGenerator` as `CUS-YYYYMM-0001` when the
caller does not supply one. A caller-supplied code is kept as-is, so an
imported or externally-issued identifier survives.

## Permissions

`customers.view`, `customers.create`, `customers.update`, `customers.delete`,
enforced by route middleware and re-checked by `CustomerPolicy`.
